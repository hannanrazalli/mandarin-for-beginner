# Beginner practice pack — provisional text review

Scope: `content/practice-dialogues.json` only; canonical lesson answers, versions,
HSK classifications and existing lesson files are unchanged. The pack contains five
short dialogues (28 turns), ten response-choice exercises with three options each,
and ten two-option listening contrasts. Suggested practice is 4 minutes per dialogue
plus 30 seconds per contrast: 25 minutes, including replay and active response time.
These are estimates, not recordings of that duration or new graded curriculum units.

All new text-bearing objects have `reviewStatus: "unverified"`. Audio is an unverified
`zh-CN` synthesis request, with `needs_audio_review: true`, slow rate 0.7, and no
recorded asset or native-voice claim. Missing device audio must have an honest fallback;
these activities do not measure speech quality. No paid service is required by the content.

The five situations cover introducing yourself, ordering at a kopitiam, asking a price,
clock time and messaging a friend. Dialogues use Standard Mandarin and direct Malay
and English meanings. 安娜 and 阿里 are illustrative personal names. 令吉 (`lìngjí`)
is the established Malaysian Mandarin name for ringgit; prices are fictional and are
not current market information. Kopitiam context does not imply that Mandarin drink
phrases reproduce dialect-based local ordering codes. 不加糖 means no added sugar;
it does not independently request milk-free or dairy-free coffee.

Pinyin preserves dictionary tones. Notes explicitly distinguish ordinary spoken changes:
`nǐ hǎo → ní hǎo`, `nǎlǐ → nálǐ`, `yī bēi → yì bēi`,
`yīgòng → yígòng`, `gěi nǐ → géi nǐ`, `yīqǐ → yìqǐ`,
and `bù yào → bú yào`. Neutral syllables are unmarked. Longer chains of third tones
are not assigned a single universal surface transcription. 个 may become neutral in
speech even when the displayed dictionary form is `gè`.

Listening contrasts use real sample characters with meanings. Most pairs differ in tone;
`七/鸡` contrasts aspiration, `林/零` contrasts final n/ng, and `绿/路` contrasts ü/u.
`四/十` explicitly changes both the initial and the tone and is not a tone-only minimal
pair. `林` and `里` are labelled honestly as character meanings/word elements rather
than claims that every isolated sample is a complete everyday utterance. All samples
are displayed with pinyin and translations for review. The correct target option tells
the app which Chinese audio text to play; a TTS mismatch is not a learner error.

Each response prompt specifies a communicative intent, leaving one matching answer.
Distractors remain grammatical Mandarin with accurate meanings; they are unsuitable
for the requested intent, not inherently invalid language. Feedback explains the mismatch.
A suggested method is to listen, respond before revealing an answer, replay, then replace
one personal detail such as name, place or time. The content does not claim DSKP/SJKC
alignment, external native-speaker approval, an HSK level or conversational fluency.

Uncertainty: no unresolved specific lexical or grammatical issue was identified in this
writing pass. All text remains unverified pending independent review; regional usage,
audio realization, neutral tones and third-tone grouping especially need listening review.
Validation checks JSON parsing, five dialogues of 2–6 turns, exact bilingual fields,
unique IDs, valid question/option references, status/audio flags and 25-minute totals.
